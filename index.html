Talking notes

Before going in

  • Ask for twenty minutes, one to one. Not a corridor conversation.
  • Bring nothing. No papers, no printouts, no phone on the table. Documents make it a case; this is a conversation.
  • Don't memorise it. Five beats: gratitude, the gap, the dark, the health sentence, the three asks. Word-perfect sounds rehearsed, and rehearsed sounds adversarial.
  • Slower than feels natural. Nervousness speeds speech up, and speed reads as agitation.
  • When he interrupts, let him finish. Answer in one or two sentences, then say "Can I finish the thought?" and pick up where she left off. Interruptions are not the enemy — a manager who argues is engaging. The failure mode is being pulled off the thread and never reaching the three asks.
  • Decide the three asks are non-negotiable — everything else can be cut if the conversation turns. Those three get said.
  • Beforehand, find whatever exists on the Romanian recognition — the application, forms, emails, anything in her file. Not to bring into the room, but so she knows how far it actually got if he asks.

— then stop talking —

The silence is the most useful part. After the three asks, say nothing. It will feel far longer than it is. People fill silences, and what they fill them with is usually closer to the real answer than anything they'd say in response to a question. If he says something vague, resist the urge to follow up — wait again.

Not in this conversation

  • The other doctor's on-call arrangement. Different matter, different forum, enormous downside here.
  • Adam, his email, or anything from it. It would turn her situation into a continuation of his.
  • Comparisons with named colleagues. Save it; it lands far harder later with dates attached.
  • The Romanian implant course as a substitute — if it comes up, it's documentation toward a delmål, not a replacement.
  • Any mention of the union, the studierektor, or escalation. Those run quietly in parallel.

Within 24 hours after

  • Send him a short summary email. Three or four lines: thanks for the time, what she understood, what was agreed, by when. Warm, not legalistic. This is what turns a conversation into a record.
  • Write down her own notes separately — what he actually said, including anything vague, while it's fresh.
  • If he committed to anything with a date, put it in the email explicitly. Undated commitments evaporate.

Innan hon går in

  • Be om tjugo minuter, enskilt. Inget korridorsamtal.
  • Ta inte med något. Inga papper, inga utskrifter, ingen telefon på bordet. Dokument gör det till ett ärende; det här är ett samtal.
  • Lär dig inte utantill. Fem hållpunkter: tacksamheten, glappet, ovissheten, hälsomeningen, de tre frågorna. Ordagrant låter inövat, och inövat låter som en anklagelse.
  • Långsammare än vad som känns naturligt. Nervositet gör talet snabbare, och snabbhet läses som upprördhet.
  • När han avbryter: låt honom prata färdigt. Svara på en eller två meningar, säg sedan ”Får jag prata klart?” och ta vid där hon slutade. Avbrott är inte fienden — en chef som invänder är engagerad. Felet är att tappa tråden och aldrig komma fram till de tre frågorna.
  • Bestäm att de tre frågorna är oförhandlingsbara — allt annat kan strykas om samtalet vänder. De tre sägs.
  • Ta reda på i förväg vad som finns om tillgodoräknandet — ansökan, blanketter, mejl, allt i hennes akt. Inte för att ta med in, utan för att veta hur långt det faktiskt kom om han frågar.
